## How we work
- No direct commits to `main`. Every change goes through a feature branch and a pull request.
- Every PR is reviewed by someone other than its author before merging.
- Every issue has one owner.
- Each member commits meaningful work every week from their own account.
- Roles rotate weekly: ISA architect, assembler lead, pipeline lead, hazard-unit lead,
  interface lead, test lead, scrum and release lead.

## Decision making
Decisions are made by [consensus / majority vote], recorded in `docs/decisions.md`.

## If someone is stuck or absent
Tell the group at least [24 hours] before a deadline. Someone else reviews or helps.
